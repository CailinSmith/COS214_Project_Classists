Detailed Description

Design Pattern: Decorator Participant: Concrete Decorator.

Author
Jordan Naidoo

Adds a ceramic pot object to plant products that customers purchase which modifies its behaviour. This impacts the price as well as the appearance of the plants in the nursery (via the getName and summary methods).
