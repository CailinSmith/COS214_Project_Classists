Detailed Description

Design Pattern: Decorator Participant: Concrete Decorator.

Author
Jordan Naidoo

Adds kraft paper wrapping to plant products that customers purchase which modifies its behaviour. This impacts the price as well as the appearance of the plants in the nursery (via the getName and summary methods). Kraft wrapping provides an eco-friendly packaging option for customers.
