\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Design Pattern\+: Command Participant\+: Client/\+Invoker. 

\begin{DoxyAuthor}{Author}
Abhay Rooplall 
\end{DoxyAuthor}
This class represents a customer in the plant shop system. It acts as the invoker in the Command pattern, sending commands to staff members to perform various operations such as checking out, checking stock, or requesting refunds. The customer maintains their shopping cart and purchase history.
