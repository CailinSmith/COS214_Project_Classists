\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Design Pattern\+: Chain of Responsibility, Observer Participant\+: Concrete Handler, Concrete Observer. 

\begin{DoxyAuthor}{Author}
Abhay Rooplall 

Edwin Kusel 

Cailin Smith 
\end{DoxyAuthor}
This class represents nursery staff members who handle plant-\/related requests such as getting plant information and checking stock. It implements the \doxylink{classStaff}{Staff} interface as part of the Chain of Responsibility pattern and acts as an observer for inventory updates.
