\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents a strawberry fruit plant. Design Pattern\+: Abstract Factory Participant\+: Concrete\+Product. 

\begin{DoxyAuthor}{Author}
Cailin Smith 
\end{DoxyAuthor}
This class represents a strawberry plant with medium moisture requirements and thinning pruning needs. Strawberries are popular fruit plants that require regular watering and pruning to produce quality berries. They are best sold in spring.
