\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Design Pattern\+: \doxylink{classDecorator}{Decorator} Participant\+: Concrete \doxylink{classDecorator}{Decorator}. 

\begin{DoxyAuthor}{Author}
Jordan Naidoo 
\end{DoxyAuthor}
Adds kraft paper wrapping to plant products that customers purchase which modifies its behaviour. This impacts the price as well as the appearance of the plants in the nursery (via the get\+Name and summary methods). Kraft wrapping provides an eco-\/friendly packaging option for customers.
