\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Design Pattern\+: \doxylink{classDecorator}{Decorator} Participant\+: Concrete \doxylink{classDecorator}{Decorator}. 

\begin{DoxyAuthor}{Author}
Jordan Naidoo 
\end{DoxyAuthor}
Adds a concrete pot object to plant products that customers purchase which modifies its behaviour. This impacts the price as well as the appearance of the plants in the nursery (via the get\+Name and summary methods).
