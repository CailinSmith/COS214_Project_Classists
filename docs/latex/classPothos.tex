\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents a \doxylink{classPothos}{Pothos} indoor plant. Design Pattern\+: Abstract Factory Participant\+: Concrete\+Product. 

\begin{DoxyAuthor}{Author}
Cailin Smith 
\end{DoxyAuthor}
This class represents a \doxylink{classPothos}{Pothos}, a popular indoor trailing plant with medium moisture requirements and thinning pruning needs. \doxylink{classPothos}{Pothos} plants are hardy and easy to care for, making them perfect for beginners. They are best sold in winter.
