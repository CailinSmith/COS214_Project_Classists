\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Design Pattern\+: Chain of Responsibility Participant\+: Concrete Handler. 

\begin{DoxyAuthor}{Author}
Abhay Rooplall 

Edwin Kusel 
\end{DoxyAuthor}
This class represents sales staff members who handle customer checkout and refund requests. It implements the \doxylink{classStaff}{Staff} interface as part of the Chain of Responsibility pattern, processing sales-\/related requests.
